# Bayan Mahfoud

Fourth-year undergraduate Artificial Intelligence student from Syria and founder and lead researcher of [MIND-B](https://mindb-qk5njhtw.manus.space/ ), a personal research laboratory I am building from the ground up.

## About me

I am building a research foundation at the intersection of artificial intelligence, NeuroAI, consciousness, and computational neuroscience.

My long-term research interest is the study of consciousness and disorders of consciousness, particularly how computational modelling, neural dynamics, and neural data analysis may help us understand conscious states and their disruption.

I am currently at the literature-study, theory-development, and foundation-building stage. I am strengthening my background in neuroscience, neural dynamics, mathematical modelling, scientific Python, machine learning, neural signal processing, and reproducible research.

My current goal is not to present a premature solution. It is to understand the problem carefully: how brain states are studied, how disorders of consciousness are assessed, what computational and neural interventions have been explored, and which scientific questions remain open.

## MIND-B

[MIND-B](https://mindb-qk5njhtw.manus.space/ ) is a personal research laboratory led by one researcher. It documents a real path from learning and implementation to early computational experiments and, eventually, original research projects.

The laboratory is currently in its foundation stage and is developing its first project. Its work is organized around research seasons, with each season focusing on a defined stage of learning, experimentation, review, or project development.

This GitHub profile supports that process by hosting code, computational experiments, technical notes, and project files when they are ready to share. The laboratory page publishes major milestones, seasons, projects, and releases, while this profile documents the technical work behind them.

An idea is not a hypothesis, and a hypothesis is not a result.

## Current research direction

I am currently studying the scientific literature on:

- Consciousness and its neural correlates
- Disorders of consciousness
- Neural dynamics and large-scale brain networks
- EEG and neural signal analysis
- Computational modelling of brain states
- NeuroAI and biologically inspired computation
- Brain stimulation and neuromodulation
- Adaptive and closed-loop neural interventions
- Reproducible computational research

The specific intervention mechanism has not been defined. My current priority is to understand the problem deeply enough to formulate a precise, testable, and scientifically responsible research hypothesis.

This profile does not present a clinical solution, treatment, or validated neural intervention.
